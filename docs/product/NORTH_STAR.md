# Travel Concierge — North Star

One-page product constitution. Keep current. If this drifts, fix here first.

## Mission

Travel Concierge is a discovery-first travel intelligence platform.

Product flow:

```
Discover → Search → Save → Plan → Optimize → Watch
```

## Non-negotiables

- The app must be useful before a trip exists.
- A Travel Idea / Saved Item is the future root object.
- Trip is one conversion path, not the prerequisite.
- Wife-wow goal: PK's wife should open the app and immediately feel it is beautiful, useful, premium, and worth browsing.

## What this means in practice

- Any product slice that does not move Discover, Save, Plan, Optimize, or Watch forward is out of scope unless it unblocks the spine.
- Any visible feature must respect Google Places authority for addable cards and must not leak diagnostics, sample data, or unsupported claims.
- Premium feel and emotional clarity are real success criteria, not afterthoughts. They are gated, not built ad hoc.
