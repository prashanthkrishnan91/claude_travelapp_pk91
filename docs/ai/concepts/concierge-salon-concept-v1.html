The Private Travel Salon · A Direction Study

A concierge room that opens onto
the place you are dreaming of.

The outside-trip concierge is not a chat box on paper. It is a salon — a cinematic room inside the Atelier world whose centrepiece is a portal: a deep window that suggests possibility before you ask, and tunes toward your destination or mood after you do. The concierge desk is fused into the base of the portal. To its right, a dossier explains what the concierge can do. After a search, a curated shortlist reveals as editorial cards you can save to your folio. Try the desk below — type a destination and a mood, or choose an invitation.

What this concept demonstrates

The outside-trip concierge as a transportive salon. A layered portal (scene, haze, bloom, grain, vignette) carries the user into a mood before they ask and tunes toward their search after. The desk is an instrument fused to the portal, not a generic form. Results reveal as editorial, verified cards.

What is deliberately absent

No blank paper page. No giant dark rectangle. No generic chat dock or help card. No placeholder gradient banner with design-note captions. No fake counts or saved totals. No SaaS dashboard chrome, and no black-cave overcorrection — warmth and paper still anchor the room.

Product truth preserved

The salon surfaces verified places and helps the user save or add them — it never books on their behalf and never invents listings. Motion is atmospheric and respects reduced-motion. This is a concept prototype only; no production app code is touched.
