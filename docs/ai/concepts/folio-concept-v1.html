Frame C

The Concierge Correspondence

Cinema stage. Concierge speaks in italic serif; you reply in marine ink. Suggestions arrive on a paper sheet that slides up — never as inline bubbles.

What this concept demonstrates

The paper world is the default chrome, with a single grain layer, ambient warmth, and a brass-foil accent system. Cinema panels are rare and framed — the trip hero, the day plate, the concierge stage. One signature accent (marine ink). Gold demoted to hairlines, dividers, and the wordmark.

What is deliberately absent

No glassmorphism in chrome. No gradient text. No filled gold buttons. No category icons or coloured badges. No exclamation marks. No skeleton loaders — empty states would use a paper-noise sheet with a slow serif "preparing your folio…" fade.

Signature primitives

The issue masthead ("Issue No. 07 · Spring 2026"). The mapline (city · → · city · → · city). The folio serial (AML · 03 · 02). The day numeral as a large italic serif. The italic editorial caption under every object. These five primitives carry the identity.
