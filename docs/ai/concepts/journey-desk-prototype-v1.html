Mood — Journey Desk ≠ Reading Room. Reading Room is a bound library of many trips (brass-warm, restful). Journey Desk is a working drafting desk for one trip: cooler marine-tinted ambient, a full-width cinematic cover band (not a small monogram plate), and a two-zone plan rail + working surface (not a shelf of volumes).

Functional ownership (unchanged): Cover = identity + global actions Brief = read-only summary Itinerary = editing / day placement Ideas = canonical idea management IdeasTray = quick placement Build = internal add/search utility

Removed: TripReadinessCockpit "Trip readiness · concierge notes" disclosure — redundant with the Brief (fixed/pending facts) and Dayboard (N of M days planned), and noisy on mobile. Concierge stays reachable from the cover's AI Concierge primary. No fabricated "notes" artifact is introduced (no durable notes data source yet).
